Append-only. Entries are added, never edited or deleted. Old entries stay as written even when later entries supersede them. This file is the sanctioned keep-both-sides merge file: every merge between tracks conflicts here, and the resolution is always to keep both sides in date order.

## 2026-09-08 · D1 · What closes a period
Decision: marking a BIR form filed closes the period it covers, for that client.
Consequence: transactions that feed a filed form are locked from edit and delete. The correction path is an amended filing (D3). Whether one filing locks the whole period for every tax type, or only the figures of that form, is decided after U1 reports how forms reference transactions.

## 2026-09-08 · D2 · What the billing is
Decision: the Portal's billing is an internal statement of account. It is not the firm's BIR-registered invoice.
Consequence: BillingCounter is not a BIR series and need not be gapless. Edit-reverts-to-Draft on a Sent billing stands. Paid remains terminal, and a Paid billing needs a snapshot of what was issued.

## 2026-09-08 · D3 · Amended returns
Decision: an amended return is a new filing. The original filing is kept, unchanged.
Consequence: the filing key becomes (client, form, period, sequence). Guardrail #4 in docs/BUILD-PLAN.md ("upsert, never duplicate") is amended when this is implemented: idempotent for the same filing, never overwriting a different one. docs/bir-integration-spec.md changes with it; that contract has no external consumer yet.

Nothing is made for a day before generation began (the day a project became recurring), so a start date in the past does not flood a project; a changed schedule affects only the occurrences not yet made; turning it off stops generation and keeps the tasks, and turning it on again starts from that day; an archived project makes nothing. `GET /projects/:key/recurrence-preview?count=5` answers the next dates (today included, 1 to 24) and, with `start`, `end` or `frequency` in the query, previews a schedule that is not saved. BIR deadlines: `GET /bir/deadlines?from=YYYY-MM&to=YYYY-MM` (BIRForms:Read, at most 24 months) answers the due dates in order, each with its forms, the period each covers and the active clients the caller may see who must file, each with a status: not_started, draft, filed_bir (the BIR e-mail attached), filed_override, filed (a form that files without the e-mail, or a filing with no confirmation on record) or overdue (the due date is before today in Asia/Manila and the return is not filed), and the id of the return when one exists. Who must file comes from the client's kind and registered tax types: VAT the 2550Q, percentage tax the 2551Q, expanded withholding the 0619E (months 1 and 2 of a quarter), 1601EQ, 2307 and 1604E, compensation withholding the 1601C, 1604C and 2316, an individual the 1701Q and one annual row that stands for the 1701 or the 1701A, a corporation the 1702Q (three quarters) and the 1702RT, the 2550Q, 1702Q and 1702RT following the client's fiscal year. The due dates, in one place (`bir-forms/deadlines/due-rules.ts`; the earlier copies call it): 0619E the 10th of the next month; 1601C the 10th of the next month, December on 15 January; 1601EQ the last day of the month after the quarter; 2550Q and 2551Q the 25th day after the quarter ends; 1701Q 15 May, 15 August, 15 November; 1702Q 60 days after the quarter ends; 1701 and 1701A 15 April; 1702RT the 15th day of the 4th month after the year ends; 1604C and 2316 31 January; 1604E 1 March; 2307 20 days after the quarter. A due date on a Saturday or Sunday moves to Monday; there is no holiday table yet, and the answer says so. The answer also lists, per client, what the client record lacks to decide (the business tax, the registered tax types, the fiscal year start), and assumes the calendar year when the fiscal year start is missing. The confirmation check's late warning and the default period of the connector still use only the four forms they used before. Built in PA8 (migration 46, project_firm_details).

## 2026-11-12 · D71 · The withholding returns take the client's e-mail and contact number when none is typed
Decision (domain owner, 12 Nov): the 0619-E, 1601-EQ and 1601-C carry an e-mail (Item 13 of the 0619-E, Item 12 of the 1601-EQ and 1601-C) and a contact number (Item 11 of the 0619-E, Item 10 of the others) typed in the draft. When the typed e-mail is blank (missing or only spaces) the return prints the client's e-mail: Client.email for a draft, and the filing snapshot's e-mail once the return is filed, exactly as the contact number already reads the client's phone (U21). A typed value always wins. One helper decides each: `contactNumberOf` and `emailOf`, side by side in `bir-forms/engine/contact-number.ts`; the print, the live preview and the connector all call them. The e-mail is optional on all three blank forms (they mark no item as required), so a return with no e-mail anywhere prints the item empty and is never refused, and the connector never asks for it; a missing contact number is still refused and still asked for (U21, U22). The connector's `portal_get_bir_form` and `portal_bir_form_requirements` answer `prints: { contactNumber, email }`, what the return will print. Nothing is written into a draft or a filed row: the value is read when the return prints. A filed return reads its sealed snapshot; on the day this was built no filed 0619-E, 1601-EQ or 1601-C existed on production, so no printed copy changed. Built in U24.

## 2026-11-12 · D72 · A client can be deleted when it holds nothing sealed or shared
Decision (domain owner, 12 Nov): a client entered by mistake or twice can be deleted permanently; archiving stays the way out for a real client. `DELETE /api/v1/clients/:clientId` with `{ confirmName }` deletes the client with everything that belongs only to it: its income and purchase transactions, its categories, its BIR form drafts and their stored preview files, its BIRFiling rows that are not filed, its receipt scans with their files and rows, its input tax assets, its tax rule, its draft invoices with their lines, its COR file, its client user profiles, its invitations, its client-scoped role grants and its staff assignments. A client user's account stays and belongs to no client afterwards. An FS report linked to it stays, unlinked (its clientId set to null; the entity facts copied onto it are kept). It is refused (409), with one sentence that names what blocks it and how many, when the client has a filed BIR return (a return whose status is not draft, a draft that holds a filing history or a BIR confirmation, or a BIRFiling row with status filed), an invoice that is not a draft (Sent, Paid or Overdue: "sent invoices"), an invoice of another client billed for it (draft or not), a linked project, or a sub-client billed under it: "This client cannot be deleted: it has 3 filed BIR returns and 2 sent invoices. Archive it instead." Filed returns are sealed records (D11, D12) and never disappear through a client delete; the seal trigger refuses it as well. A linked project only blocks; it is not unlinked. `confirmName` must equal the client's registered name (a company's registered name, an individual's "LAST, FIRST MIDDLE", the Portal name when those fields are empty), with case and runs of spaces folded; anything else, or none, is 400 "Type the client's name exactly to delete it.", asked before the blockers are told. Everything is one transaction that first locks the client and its returns, filings and invoices, counts again, deletes, and writes the audit event `client.deleted` (who, the registered name and TIN, the counts removed per kind, the FS reports unlinked; never the records) in the same transaction. The stored files (the COR, then the drafts' files, then the receipt photos) are removed after the commit; a file the store will not remove is logged and listed in the answer as `filesNotRemoved`, and the delete stands. `GET /api/v1/clients/:clientId/delete-check` answers `{ canDelete, nameToType, blockers: [{ kind, count, sentence }], willDelete: [{ kind, count }], willUnlink: [{ kind, count }] }`, the same counts DELETE then answers as `{ deleted, unlinked, filesNotRemoved }`; only kinds with something in them are listed, in the order of the lists in `@portal/shared` (`CLIENT_DELETE_BLOCKER_KINDS`, `CLIENT_DELETE_KINDS`, `CLIENT_UNLINK_KINDS`). Both routes ask Clients:Delete held firm-wide; by default only Super Admin holds it (the seed took it off Manager, and the seed runs on every deploy); client users never. Another firm's client is 404 "Client not found". The connector has no delete tool. Built in U25 (no migration).
